- Cream paper background, bold black text, red the ONLY accent colour.
- Hard cuts. Flat stills: no Ken Burns, no zoom (build with KIT_STILL_ZOOM=0).
- Tiny grey source credit bottom-left on nearly every shot. Channel watermark bottom-right.
- End on the closing question card. No baked end card, no subscribe CTA in the video (cut it from narration if it is there). YouTube end-screen buttons are added in Studio at upload.
- Pacing: see [pacing-short-cards](pacing-short-cards.md).
