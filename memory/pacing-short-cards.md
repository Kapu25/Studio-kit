name: pacing-short-cards
label: Pacing: no text card over 5 s
date: 2026-11-13
source: owner
---
- No static text card holds longer than 5 seconds (6-7 s is the hard upper limit for a single sentence card).
- Opening = short beats: text card (1-5 s) -> clip / photo / graphic -> next short text card.
- Never leave one static card up through a long stretch of narration. Cut to clips, photos or interface graphics (name cards, lower-thirds) between text cards.
- Supersedes any older note that allowed chapter cards of 5-8 s.
